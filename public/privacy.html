Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Icon Library has no user accounts and does not ask for personal data. This page explains what is stored on your device, which outside services the page talks to, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Icon Library (iconlibrary.io) is run by André M. Ferreira, who is responsible for this site and for the processing of data described on this page.

What is stored in your browser

The site keeps a few preferences in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are not sent to our server:

  • the scratchpad text (icon-library:scratchpad);
  • the chosen size, stroke and color (icon-library:style);
  • the light or dark theme (icon-library:theme);
  • your supporter code and its end date, if you bought a coffee (icon-library:supporter);
  • the language you chose (icon-library:lang).

You can erase them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The site itself sets no cookies; advertising cookies are described below.

Server logs

Like any website, the server receives technical data with each request — IP address, requested page, date and time, and browser type. It is used only to keep the site running and secure, and is kept for a short period in rotating logs.

Third-party services

To show the icons, your browser downloads them directly from these services, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of the request:

  • unpkg — icon data for all four sets;
  • lucide.dev — the categories of the Lucide icons.

The icon editor runs entirely in your browser. Its Open in Lucide Studio button, shown for Lucide and Feather icons, opens Lucide Studio, an independent site with its own privacy policy, and sends it the icon you are editing.

Buying a coffee (Stripe)

Payments for “Buy me a coffee” are processed by Stripe, which receives your payment details under its own privacy policy; they never pass through this site. To give you the ad-free period, the server keeps only the Stripe payment session ID, the amount, the payment date, the generated supporter code, its end date and how many devices used it — no name, e-mail or card data. A renewal stores the new payment’s session ID, amount and date, linked to the same code. These records are kept as long as needed to honor the benefit and for the developer's financial records.

Publishing to Iconlibrary

Supporters can publish icons to Iconlibrary, the community set. For each submission the server stores the icon (its SVG), the name, the categories, the optional credit you type, your supporter code and the submission and review dates. Once approved, the icon, its name and the credit are public and available to everyone under the MIT license. To remove an icon you published, use the contact below.

Bug reports

When you send a bug report from the form in the footer, the server keeps your message, the e-mail address if you typed one (only to reply to you), and what the page sends along to help reproduce the problem: its address, language, browser (user agent) and window size. Your IP address is not stored with it. Reports are read only by the site owner and are deleted once they are no longer needed. If you open an issue on GitHub instead, it is public and follows GitHub's own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad and to measure ad performance.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through Google's consent message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under Brazil's LGPD or the European GDPR — you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since Icon Library does not collect personal data beyond the technical logs above, most requests will concern the advertising cookies, which you control through the Google links above and your browser settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy or requests about your data, contact André M. Ferreira by opening an issue in the project's GitHub repository.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
