Articles

Notes from real work with icons: what I tried, what didn't fit and why, with the SVG at the end so you can use the result right away. Each article starts from a concrete problem, like a feature that no ready-made icon could represent, and shows the path from the first search to the final drawing.

The articles are written in English and Portuguese, the two languages I can review myself. New ones are added here as they are published, newest first.
